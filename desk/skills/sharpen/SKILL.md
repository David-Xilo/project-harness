# Sharpen

Before starting, restate in your own words what the operator's goal is and the problem
behind it, and what done looks like to them. Keep it short, and name anything you are
unsure of so they can correct it. When they only asked for the restatement ("in your own
words"), stop there and let them correct it before you act.

Then ask:

- How would a top expert approach this task: their raw sources, their real methods, and
  an outside check that the result is right?
- What is the operator really after?
- Is the task over- or underspecified?

Loosen constraints that don't serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Leave
open the choices that are the operator's to make, instead of deciding them. Fold in only
what makes the work better, not bigger, but always keep one outside check that the
result is right. Beyond the restatement, don't narrate the framing, and don't hand back a rewritten prompt: do the
work.
